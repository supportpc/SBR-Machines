SBR Machines - Header Hero Videos
=====================================

Place your 2 video files in this folder with either of the following naming formats:

Option 1 (Recommended):
- video-1.mp4 (First video to autoplay)
- video-2.mp4 (Second video to autoplay after video-1 finishes)

Option 2:
- hero-video-1.mp4
- hero-video-2.mp4

Format: MP4 (H.264/AAC for best cross-browser compatibility)
Auto-play behavior:
1. Video 1 autoplays immediately when the page loads.
2. When Video 1 reaches the end, the slider smoothly transitions to Slide 2.
3. Video 2 plays from start to finish.
4. When Video 2 finishes, it loops back to Slide 1 and repeats the sequence continuously.
